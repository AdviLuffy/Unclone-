The Threat Vector

Trust the voice.
Verify the person.

Generative voice cloning has weaponized executive communications. Using under three seconds of reference audio, attackers synthesize convincing vocal replicas of corporate signatories to initiate fraudulent wire settlements and breach high-value systems.

Synthetic Voice Injection

Fine-tuned neural vocoders mimic vocal cadence, intonation, and breath patterns with high fidelity, rendering traditional phone authorization insecure.

Audio Replay & Spoofing

Even genuine audio can be harvested from public earnings calls or media appearances. Without dynamic spoken challenge verification, recorded clips bypass biometric matchers.

Zero-Trust Pipeline

Three checks.
One decision.

ALIBI requires every authorization attempt to satisfy three independent layers of verification. Neither genuine acoustics nor speaker resemblance alone is sufficient.

01 // DETECTION

Aletheia Deepfake Engine

Analyzes acoustic fingerprints, phase inconsistencies, and synthetic generation artifacts. FAKE audio triggers an immediate block and incident containment.

02 // BIOMETRICS

Eidos Voice Biometrics

Matches speaker acoustic vectors against enrolled executive profiles. Genuine human speech that fails identity matching is blocked as an impostor.

03 // LIVENESS

Dynamic Nonce Challenge

Enforces a fresh, cryptographically generated spoken phrase evaluated via real-time speech recognition to guarantee caller presence and eliminate replays.
